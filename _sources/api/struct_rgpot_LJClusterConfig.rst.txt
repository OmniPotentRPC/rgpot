Width (Angstrom) of the C^2 quintic switch (:ref:`PairSwitch.hpp <doxid-_pair_switch_8hpp_source>`) that takes each pair term to zero over [cutoff - switch_width, cutoff]. 0 keeps the shifted truncation, whose force jumps at the cutoff.
