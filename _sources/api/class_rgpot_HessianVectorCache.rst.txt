Detailed Documentation
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Centre forces and counters for Hessian-vector products.

Safe to share between threads; the lock covers the cached centre, not the force calls. The potential passed to each call must be the one the cache was filled with, or ``:ref:`invalidate() <doxid-classrgpot_1_1_hessian_vector_cache_1a11c6b78fccbaded2ebb959abedf00a19>``` must be called in between.

	template <ForceKernel Pot>
	:ref:`HvpCentre<doxid-structrgpot_1_1_hvp_centre>` centre(Pot& pot, const :ref:`ForceInput<doxid-structrgpot_1_1_force_input>`& in)

Energy and forces at *in*.

Served from the cached centre when its positions, species and cell carry the same bits, otherwise one force call that becomes the centre. A search that needs the centre forces asks here, so the products that follow reuse them.

	void apply(Pot& pot, const :ref:`ForceInput<doxid-structrgpot_1_1_force_input>`& in, const double* v, double* hv, double step = 0.0, :ref:`HvpScheme<doxid-namespacergpot_1a29b200eb6813e0ff47a0671f34e9a1b3>` scheme = :ref:`HvpScheme::Auto<doxid-namespacergpot_1a29b200eb6813e0ff47a0671f34e9a1b3a06b9281e396db002010bde1de57262eb>`)

Write :math:`H v` to *hv*.

*v* and *hv* hold ``3*in``.nAtoms doubles and must not overlap. *step* 0 selects ``kHvpDefaultStep``. A zero direction gives a zero product without a call.
