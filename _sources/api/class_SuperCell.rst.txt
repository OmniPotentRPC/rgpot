Detailed Documentation
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The :ref:`SuperCell <doxid-class_super_cell>` defines the size, shape and boundary condx of a simulation.

The :ref:`SuperCell <doxid-class_super_cell>` serves two purposes. The most important is the computational box of a simulation: it defines the size, shape and boundary conditions of the simulation. A second use is for the unit cell of the lattice in the elements of a Quasicontinuum simulation.
