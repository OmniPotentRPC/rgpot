class rgpot::nlist::Walk
========================

.. toctree::
	:hidden:

:ref:`Walk <doxid-classrgpot_1_1nlist_1_1_walk>` pairs for one central atom, or one cluster of four, at a time into caller storage. The walk does not keep a list of the whole system. It borrows ``positions`` and ``box9`` until it is destroyed. ``pull`` writes at most ``dest.first.size()`` pairs and returns how many it wrote. ``done`` is set when the geometry has been exhausted. A requested field whose span is too short throws :ref:`Error <doxid-classrgpot_1_1nlist_1_1_error>` and does not advance. One walk is used by one thread.
