Detailed Documentation
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

A host's half neighbour list over its atoms and their copies.

:ref:`Atoms <doxid-class_atoms>` [0, nOwned) are the host's own and [nOwned, nAtoms) are copies. Row k belongs to the owned atom i = rows[k], whose neighbours are neighbours[i][0 .. count[i]): the layout of a LAMMPS NeighList (ilist, numneigh, firstneigh), with ``count`` and ``neighbours`` indexed by atom. ``indexMask`` clears any bits a host sets in an entry besides the index (LAMMPS NEIGHMASK).

A pair of owned atoms is listed once, in either row. A pair of an owned atom and a copy is listed in the owned atom's row. With ``newton``, only one of the two hosts that hold such a pair lists it, and the stages write the copy's share as well for the host to add to its owner (LAMMPS newton_pair on). Without it both hosts list the pair and each writes its own atom. Entries beyond the cutoff are skipped, so a list built with a skin works. A copy sits at its image, so the separation of a pair is the plain difference of its positions.
