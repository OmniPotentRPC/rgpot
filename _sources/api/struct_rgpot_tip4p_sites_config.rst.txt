struct rgpot_tip4p_sites_config
===============================

.. toctree::
	:hidden:

Oxygen Lennard-Jones plus Coulomb on rigid TIP4P sites. ``sites`` and ``gradient`` are molecule-major O, H, H, M, three coordinates each. ``use_epsilon == 0`` uses ``c6`` and ``c12``. ``use_epsilon != 0`` uses ``epsilon`` and ``sigma``.
