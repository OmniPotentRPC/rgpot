enum rgpot::nlist::Image
========================

How the minimum image is formed. Round is the orthorhombic truncation used by the pair kernels. Half is one correction about the half-box, used after coordinates have been wrapped into the cell. General is :ref:`MinimumImage <doxid-classrgpot_1_1nlist_1_1_minimum_image>`, for a non-orthogonal cell or a periodic side shorter than the search.
