Detailed Documentation
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Represents atoms seen through the periodic boundaries.

The :ref:`Image <doxid-struct_image>` structure is used by :ref:`NeighborList <doxid-class_neighbor_list>` to represent atoms seen through the periodic boundary conditions.
