:ref:`Iceland <doxid-structforcefields_1_1zhu__philpott__parameters_1_1_iceland>` parameterisation. In this parameterisation, the Lennard-Jones interactions between platinum and water have been weakened to match the binding energy found by DFT calculation which is 0.3 eV for one molecule on a (100) surface.
