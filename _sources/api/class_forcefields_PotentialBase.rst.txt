	double getSwitchingWidth() const

Width of the switching zone. In the switching zone the potential is replaced by :math:`V=U\times S(x)` where *U* is the real potential *V* the switching potential and *S* the switching function. Variable x=0 at the beginning of the switching zone and x=1 at the end the function S is :math:`S=2x^3-3x^2+1` which has the following properties: S(0)=1, S(1)=0, S'(0)=0 and S'(1)=0. The switching when the distance between the two molecules is getCutoff()- :ref:`getSwitchingWidth() <doxid-classforcefields_1_1_potential_base_1a3e28d111b296526628fa0fdffe831eb7>` and ends when the distance is getCutoff();
