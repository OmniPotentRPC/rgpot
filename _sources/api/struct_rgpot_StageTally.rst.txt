Detailed Documentation
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

What forceStage adds up besides the forces.

The host's share of the energy and of the virial W = -V sigma, in the Voigt order of :ref:`stress.hpp <doxid-stress_8hpp_source>` (xx yy zz yz xz xy): all of a pair that only this host lists, half of a pair that both hosts list, and the site energy of each owned atom. Per-atom arrays, when set, take the split of :ref:`PerAtom.hpp <doxid-_per_atom_8hpp_source>` over nAtoms entries; a copy's entries are written only with HalfList::newton, for the host to add to its owner.
