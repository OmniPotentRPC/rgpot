C++ engine-backed potentials¶

These potentials call an external program or library: a quantum-chemistry engine, a tight-binding library, a machine-learned model or a socket peer. Every class derives from rgpot::Potential<Derived>, except the dlopen frontends XTBDlopen and MetatomicDlopen, which have no base class and declare forceImpl themselves. The members they inherit (forceImpl, forceBatch, caps, the call operator) are described with the base class; this page lists only the members each class declares itself. The in-process kernels are in C++ potentials.

Unless an entry says otherwise, a class takes positions and the cell in Angstrom (cell vectors as rows) and returns the energy in eV and the forces in eV/Angstrom. Engines that work in atomic units get positions converted with units::ANGSTROM_TO_BOHR, the energy with units::HARTREE_TO_EV and gradients with units::NEG_GRAD_TO_FORCE (minus Hartree/bohr to eV/Angstrom), all from CppCore/rgpot/units.hpp.

How each class finds its engine¶

The dlopen frontends try a list of library names in order. A bare name is resolved by the dynamic linker search path. CP2KPot, CPMDPot, MOPACPot, NWChemPot, XTBDlopen and MetatomicDlopen stop at the first library that opens and then check its symbols, so a library with missing symbols ends the search. LammpsPot, GromacsPot and OpenMMPot skip a library that lacks the symbols and try the next name.

XTBDlopen and MetatomicDlopen also throw from the constructor, so available() is true for every object they construct.

CP2K¶

rgpot::CP2KPot¶

CP2KPot();
explicit CP2KPot(const ::CP2KParams::Reader &params);
Inputs

optional CP2KParams (Cap’n Proto); the default constructor uses the schema defaults.

Units

positions and cell in Angstrom, energy in eV, forces in eV/Angstrom. The class converts positions and cell to bohr, and the shim’s Hartree energy and Hartree/bohr forces back. The shim returns forces, so there is no sign change.

Outputs

a potential that runs CP2K Quickstep through the libcp2kc_rgpot shim.

Errors

the constructors do not throw when the shim is missing; see loadError. A force throws rgpot::CP2KScfError (a std::runtime_error) when the SCF does not converge, on every rank of the calculator. Any other failure throws std::runtime_error: shim not loaded, a bad inputTemplate, nAtoms of zero, a null buffer, a refused calculator binding, the shim refusing the deck or the orbital key, or a nonzero shim status.

The shim must report cp2kc_abi_version() equal to CP2KC_ABI_VERSION and export the session functions, and cp2kc_available(), when exported, must be nonzero. The deck is rendered from the params (or from inputTemplate, which must contain both @RGPOT_COORD@ and @RGPOT_CELL@) on the first force and again whenever the sequence of atomic numbers changes. A non-empty dataDir sets CP2K_DATA_DIR before the session starts. The build option is -Dwith_cp2k=enabled and it needs -Dwith_rpc=true.

CPMD¶

rgpot::CPMDPot¶

CPMDPot();
explicit CPMDPot(const ::CPMDParams::Reader &params);
Inputs

optional CPMDParams (Cap’n Proto); the default constructor uses the schema defaults.

Units

positions in Angstrom, energy in eV, forces in eV/Angstrom, stress in eV/Angstrom3. With the session ABI, rgpot sends a ForceInput tagged angstrom and eV and the engine returns those units. With the one-shot ABI, positions go in Angstrom and the class converts the Hartree energy and the Hartree/bohr gradient. It also converts a Hartree/bohr3stress when the engine exports cpmdc_last_stress and reports a valid tensor.

Outputs

a potential that runs CPMD through libcpmdc.

Errors

the constructors do not throw when the engine is missing. A force throws std::runtime_error when the engine is not available, nAtoms is zero, a buffer is null, the calculator binding was refused, the orbital key is refused, the engine reports failure, or the returned PotentialResult is malformed or has the wrong force count.

The engine must export the feature discovery functions (cpmdc_feature_count, cpmdc_feature_table, cpmdc_feature_find) and either the session result ABI or cpmdc_energy_gradient with cpmdc_set_params. A non-empty cpmdRoot sets CPMD_ROOT. The one-shot ABI does not receive the cell. The class is built when -Dwith_rpc=true.

rgpot::XTBPot¶

XTBPot();
explicit XTBPot(const XTBConfig &config);
Inputs

config, an rgpot::XTBConfig; the default constructor, where there is one, uses its defaults.

Units

positions in Angstrom, energy in eV, forces in eV/Angstrom, stress in eV/Angstrom3. The class sends positions and cell in bohr and converts the Hartree energy, the Hartree/bohr gradient and the Hartree virial divided by the cell volume. Outputs

a potential that calls the linked libxtb C API.

Errors

std::runtime_error from the constructor when the xtb environment, calculator or results cannot be created. A force throws std::runtime_error when the handles are missing, xtb_newMolecule fails, the single point fails, or the energy or gradient is not finite. After such a failure the handles are replaced.

A new composition rebuilds the molecule. A force starts the SCC from the previous wavefunction when the composition is unchanged, no atom moved further than warmMove(), and the HOMO-LUMO gap of that wavefunction is at least warmMinGap(). A single point started that way that does not converge, or whose gap falls below warmMinGap(), is run again from xtb’s own guess. GFN-FF has no orbitals, so its gap never blocks reuse. The first single point of a force runs at most 48 SCC iterations, or max_iterations when that is smaller. An SCC that does not converge is retried at 1000, 3000 and 5000 K (those above the configured temperature) and then evaluated again at the configured temperature. The build option is -Dwith_xtb=true.

i-PI socket¶

rgpot::IPIConfig¶

Inputs

address (default empty), timeout_s (default 30.0). address is unix:/absolute/path, unix:<name> (the i-PI default path with prefix ipi_), tcp:<host>:<port> or tcp:<port> (host 127.0.0.1); port 0 lets the kernel choose.

Units

timeout_s in seconds; it covers one whole force call, including the wait for the driver to connect.

Outputs

a plain struct read by IPIPot.

Errors

IPIPot throws std::invalid_argument for an address without a unix: or tcp: prefix, an empty UNIX address, a UNIX name containing /, a UNIX path too long for the socket address, a missing or out-of-range port, or a timeout_s that is negative or not finite. It throws std::runtime_error when the host does not resolve or the socket cannot be bound or listened on.

rgpot::IPIPot¶

explicit IPIPot(const IPIConfig &config);
Inputs

config, an rgpot::IPIConfig; the default constructor, where there is one, uses its defaults.

Units

positions and cell in Angstrom, energy in eV, forces in eV/Angstrom, stress in eV/Angstrom3. On the wire the class sends bohr (the cell with lattice vectors in columns, and its inverse) and converts the driver’s Hartree energy, Hartree/bohr forces and Hartree virial; the stress is -W/V. timeout_s is in seconds and covers one whole force call, including the wait for the driver to connect.

Outputs

a potential that binds the socket at construction and evaluates through one i-PI driver.

Errors

std::invalid_argument from the constructor for a bad address, port or timeout or a UNIX path that is too long; std::runtime_error when the socket cannot be bound or listened on. A force throws std::invalid_argument for missing buffers, zero atoms, more atoms than the protocol limit, non-finite positions or cell, or a singular cell, and std::runtime_error on a timeout, a disconnect, an unexpected header, a driver that does not become READY, or a mismatched atom count. After a failed exchange the connection is closed and the next force waits for a new driver.

Atomic numbers are not sent; the driver keeps its own species list. The destructor sends EXIT. The force call does not write ForceOut::stress; read it with copyStress. The build option is -Dwith_ipi; its default, auto, builds the class everywhere except Windows.

#include "rgpot/IPIPot/IPIPot.hpp"

#include <array>
#include <cstdio>
#include <vector>

int main() {
  rgpot::IPIPot pot({.address = "tcp:127.0.0.1:0", .timeout_s = 60.0});
  std::printf("driver should connect to %s\n", pot.endpoint().c_str());

  rgpot::types::AtomMatrix positions(2, 3);
  positions(0, 0) = 0.0;
  positions(0, 1) = 0.0;
  positions(0, 2) = 0.0;
  positions(1, 0) = 0.74;
  positions(1, 1) = 0.0;
  positions(1, 2) = 0.0;
  std::vector<int> numbers{1, 1};
  std::array<std::array<double, 3>, 3> box{{{10, 0, 0}, {0, 10, 0}, {0, 0, 10}}};
  auto [energy, forces, variance] = pot(positions, numbers, box);
  double stress[9];
  if (pot.copyStress(stress))
