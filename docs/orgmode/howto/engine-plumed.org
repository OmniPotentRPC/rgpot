* PLUMED

=RGPOT= is a PLUMED action that builds an rgpot potential by name and evaluates
it on a group of atoms.
Its value is the potential energy, and its derivatives are the forces and the
virial, so =BIASVALUE= applies the rgpot potential as a bias.
Through =gmx mdrun -plumed= that is the whole potential of a GROMACS run; see
the GROMACS page.
It was built and tested against PLUMED 2.9.2 with Open MPI and PLUMED 2.10.2
from conda-forge.

** Build the plugin

meson builds =libRgpotPlumed.so= when pkg-config finds =plumed=:

#+begin_src bash
meson setup bld -Dwith_plumed=enabled -Dwith_eam_table=true
meson compile -C bld
#+end_src

=-Dwith_plumed=enabled= makes a missing PLUMED an error; the default skips the
plugin.
It links =libplumedKernel=.
When =plumed config has mpi= reports an MPI kernel, the plugin is compiled with
=__PLUMED_HAS_MPI=, without which the kernel's communicator symbols do not
resolve.
=with_eam_table= is only needed for the =EAMTable= potential.
The build also makes =rgpot-eval=, which evaluates the same named potentials on
one geometry and is what the tests compare against.

** Use it

#+begin_src text
LOAD FILE=/path/to/libRgpotPlumed.so
ene: RGPOT ...
  POTENTIAL=EAMTable OPTIONS=file=CuNi.eam.alloy
  SPECIES1=1-72 SPECIES2=73-108 ELEMENTS=Cu,Ni
...
b: BIASVALUE ARG=ene
PRINT ARG=ene FILE=COLVAR
#+end_src

| Keyword      | Meaning                                                  |
|--------------+----------------------------------------------------------|
| =POTENTIAL=  | rgpot potential name, as for =pair_coeff * * Name=       |
| =OPTIONS=    | its options as =key=value= pairs, in Angstrom and eV     |
| =ATOMS=      | the atoms                                                |
| =SPECIESn=   | the atoms of species n, instead of =ATOMS=               |
| =ELEMENTS=   | one symbol per atom, per =SPECIESn= group, or for all    |
| =CUTOFF=     | cutoff in PLUMED length, used when =OPTIONS= has none    |
| =NOPBC=      | treat the atoms as a cluster in vacuum                   |
| =VACUUM=     | with =NOPBC=, the gap between images, in PLUMED length   |
| =VIRIAL=     | =AUTO= (default) or =FD=                                 |

The names and options are those of the LAMMPS page: =LJ=, =Morse=, =SuttonChen=,
=EAMTable=, =SWSi1985=, =TersoffSi=, =EMT=, =EAMCell=, and with the Fortran
potentials =SWSi=, =EDIP=, =LenoskySi=, =EAMAl=, =FeHe= and =CuH2=.
=OPTIONS= takes commas or, inside braces, spaces: =OPTIONS=u0=0.0104,psi=3.40=
and =OPTIONS={u0=0.0104 psi=3.40}= are the same.
A relative =file= is opened from the directory PLUMED runs in.
Without =CUTOFF= or a =cutoff= option, a potential keeps its own default.
=ELEMENTS= is required with =POTENTIAL=.
The atoms reach the potential in the order of =ATOMS=, or =SPECIES1= then
=SPECIES2= and so on.

These inputs stop PLUMED with a message: an unknown name, option or element
symbol, an option without an equals sign, a wrong number of =ELEMENTS=, =ATOMS=
together with =SPECIESn=, an atom given twice, =NOPBC= without a gap, and a
periodic run without a box.
A name that is not known lists the ones this build has.

** Units

=OPTIONS= are always Angstrom and eV, whatever PLUMED's units are.
=CUTOFF=, =VACUUM=, the value and its derivatives are in PLUMED's units: by
default nanometres and =kJ/mol=, and whatever =UNITS= sets otherwise.
The constructor converts once, with =rgpot_md_nm_per_angstrom= (0.1) and
=rgpot_md_kj_per_ev= (96.48533212331002, \(e N_A / 1000\) from the exact SI
constants) against PLUMED's units.
PLUMED's own eV is 96.48530749925792 =kJ/mol=.
Under =UNITS ENERGY=eV= the value therefore reads \(2.552 \times 10^{-7}\)
higher than the rgpot energy in eV; the test measures that ratio.
Divide a =kJ/mol= value by 96.48533212331002 to get the rgpot energy exactly.

** Cell and virial

Without =NOPBC= the cell is PLUMED's box, lattice vectors as rows, and the atoms
are wrapped into it before the potential sees them.
A frame without a box stops with an error.
With =NOPBC= the atoms are placed in an orthorhombic cell that leaves =VACUUM=
between periodic images, and the box derivatives follow from the positions.
=VACUUM= must exceed the potential's cutoff; it defaults to =CUTOFF= when that
is given.
The positions are used as they are, so a cluster split across a periodic
boundary is not made whole.

With a box, =VIRIAL=AUTO= takes the box derivatives as \(-V\sigma\) from the
potential's Cauchy stress.
A potential without a stress, such as =EAMAl=, and =VIRIAL=FD= use central
differences of the energy over six strains of the cell of \(10^{-5}\), twelve
more evaluations each step.

** MPI

Rank 0 of PLUMED's communicator evaluates the potential and broadcasts the
energy, forces and virial.
The evaluation is serial, and a domain decomposed run gives the result of the
serial one.

** Original keywords

Without =POTENTIAL=, =RGPOT= is the shifted Lennard-Jones potential of =U0=,
=PSI= and =CUTOFF=, in PLUMED's units, with defaults 1, 1 and 8.
Atoms take atomic number 1.
=ene: RGPOT ATOMS=1,2 U0=96.48533212331002 CUTOFF=0.8 PSI=0.1= is a 1 eV well
with a 1 Angstrom zero crossing and an 8 Angstrom cutoff in GROMACS units.
=U0= and =PSI= stop with an error when =POTENTIAL= is given.

** Tests

=plumed_driver= runs the original keywords on a dimer.
=plumed_rgpot_action= runs six cases through =plumed driver=: Lennard-Jones in a
triclinic cell in nm and =kJ/mol= and again in Angstrom and eV, Morse,
=EAMTable= CuNi through =SPECIES1= and =SPECIES2=, =EAMAl= and a SuttonChen Pt13
cluster under =NOPBC=.
One trajectory per case holds the geometry, coordinates moved by \(\pm 10^{-4}\)
Angstrom and the six strains of \(\pm 10^{-5}\), so one run gives the energies
to difference, and =DUMPDERIVATIVES= gives the derivatives.

The largest deviations, relative to the largest energy, force or virial
component, were the same on PLUMED 2.9.2 and 2.10.2:

| Case            | energy  | forces  | FD forces | FD virial | \(-V\sigma\) |
|-----------------+---------+---------+-----------+-----------+--------------|
| LJ, nm          | 1e-15   | 2e-14   | 9e-9      | 5e-9      | 9e-15        |
| LJ, Angstrom    | 5e-16   | 2e-14   | 9e-9      | 5e-9      | 3e-15        |
| Morse           | 6e-16   | 3e-14   | 5e-9      | 4e-8      | 2e-14        |
| EAMTable CuNi   | 5e-16   | 4e-14   | 5e-8      | 2e-8      | 2e-14        |
| EAMAl           | 2e-15   | 2e-13   | 3e-8      | 8e-8      | no stress    |
| SuttonChen Pt13 | 5e-16   | 4e-15   | 1e-8      | 6e-10     | 4e-15        |

Energy and forces are against =rgpot-eval= on the same geometry.
\(-V\sigma\) is from the =rgpot-eval= stress; for the cluster it is computed in
a 40 Angstrom box.
=VIRIAL=FD= agreed with the stress to within 4e-8.
PLUMED's =--debug-forces= agreed to within 3e-5; it prints six decimals.
On PLUMED 2.9.2 with Open MPI, the same trajectories on 2 and 3 ranks with
=--debug-dd yes= wrote the same =COLVAR=, derivatives and applied forces as the
serial run, digit for digit.
The test also checks that twelve malformed inputs stop with their message.
