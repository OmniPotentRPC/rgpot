* OpenMP gathers

=with_eam_al_openmp= threads the embedded-atom aluminium gather and the
Fortran Stillinger-Weber and Tersoff gathers. It is off by default.
The neighbour builder has the same layout either way. The partner-order
index is empty when the gather is not threaded.

#+begin_src bash
meson setup bbdir -Dwith_eam_al_openmp=true -Dwith_fortran_pots=enabled \
  --buildtype=release
meson compile -C bbdir
#+end_src

The thread count is =OMP_NUM_THREADS=. One thread, and a cell below 32
atoms, stays on the serial routine. =rgpot.has_eam_al_openmp= is false
when the option was off, and the benchmark rows that need it are skipped.

** Determinism

The forces and the energy are the same bits at every thread count. A
tie in the partner order keeps the earlier pair, which is the order the
serial sort writes. Each atom writes only its own force column. The
energy is the sum of the per-atom values afterwards, in atom order.

** Measured scaling

Warm call, identical positions, one 8-core run with
=OMP_PROC_BIND=close= and =OMP_PLACES=cores=. Times are milliseconds.

| threads | EAM Al 32768 | SW 32768 | Tersoff 32768 |
|---------+--------------+----------+---------------|
|       1 |         64.1 |     5.55 |          17.4 |
|       2 |         37.6 |     3.21 |          9.14 |
|       4 |         20.9 |     1.65 |          5.01 |
|       8 |         11.4 |    0.933 |          2.89 |

Eight threads are 5.6×, 5.9×, and 6.0× the one-thread call.

The aluminium neighbour search at 32768 atoms, cutoff 7.1 Å and skin
0.71 Å, is 176 ms on one thread and 53 ms on eight. The eight-thread
walk is 29 ms. The slowest thread owns cells 729–1000 and 610343 pairs
and takes 27.5 ms; the lightest owns 484142 pairs and takes 21.3 ms.
Cell binning is 0.50 ms, the stencil is 1.17 ms, and the allocation is
0.02 ms. The gather copy is 16.3 ms: 223 MB on eight threads, 13.5 GB/s.
A copy of those same bytes, already resident, is 3.1 ms at 71 GB/s.
The 16.3 ms is the fresh mapping of that buffer, not a serial loop.
