* Metatomic and the engine ABI

A metatomic or UMA model is not linked into =librgpot=.
The host dlopens a library that exports =rgpot_engine_create= and
=rgpot_engine_force=, then passes positions in Angstrom, a row-major
cell, and atomic numbers.
Energy comes back in eV and forces in eV/Angstrom.
=rgpot_engine_force_batch= evaluates several systems that share one
composition.
=rgpot_engine_last_error= copies the last sentence into a caller buffer
and returns its length.

The in-tree LJ engine, =liblj_engine.so=, is that same ABI with a
24-byte config of three float64 values: =u0=, cutoff, =psi=.
A null config is =u0= 1 eV, cutoff 15 Angstrom, =psi= 1 Angstrom.
The meson test =lj_engine= dlopens it and compares the energy and the
forces, bit for bit, with =rgpot_lj_eval=.
A metatomic engine is selected by which library is opened, not by a
different host call.

=rgpot_engine_abi_version= is the major.
=rgpot_engine_abi_stamp= reports minor and layout.
A host that only calls =rgpot_engine_abi_version= keeps working across
a minor bump.
The stamp and =rgpot_engine_abi_compatible= refuse a different major
or a different layout.
