Neighbour lists and potentials no longer hang, crash or read through a null pointer when a system has no pair inside the cutoff (no atoms, one atom, or atoms all farther apart than the cutoff), whether on the first call or after a call with pairs, and on open or periodic cells. A full brute-force vesin list could hang, the vendored Fortran vesin interface left its arrays disassociated, `EMTPot` crashed on an empty first system, and `rgpot_nlist_fortran_build` rejected an empty system.
