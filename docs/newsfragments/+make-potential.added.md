`rgpot::makePotential` (`rgpot/PotentialFactory.hpp`) builds the potential a `PotentialConfig` names, from a reader or from the flat-array bytes, and returns it as `std::unique_ptr<rgpot::PotentialBase>`. The in-process arms use the backends compiled into the build. The `orca` arm builds the new `rgpot::ORCACPot`, a frontend of the split orcac shim on the pattern of `MOPACPot`: it dlopens `liborcac` (`ORCAC_LIBRARY`, `RGPOT_ORCAC_ENGINE`, then `liborcac.so.1` and `liborcac.so`), checks `orcac_c_abi_version` 1 and creates one session from the whole `PotentialConfig`, so `liborcac` lowers `common`. The `metatomic`, `uma` and `plugin` arms, and `xtb` in a build without `-Dwith_xtb`, load an engine library over the generic engine C ABI as an `rgpot::EnginePot`; `plugin` opens `libraryPath`, or `lib<pluginName>_engine` through `RGPOT_<PLUGINNAME>_ENGINE`, with `opaquePayload` as the configuration. An arm the build cannot construct throws `std::invalid_argument` naming the arm and the option that enables it.
