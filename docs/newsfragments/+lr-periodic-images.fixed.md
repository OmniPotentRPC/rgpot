`rgpot::lr::DualList` now builds its half-shell candidate lists through `rgpot::nlist::Builder`, enumerating every periodic image inside the cutoff once (including one canonical direction for self images when the cutoff exceeds a cubic or triclinic cell vector), and filters candidates on the active OpenMP team while skipping the distance pass when coordinates and the cell are unchanged.
`rgpot::nlist::Builder::update` keeps a stored candidate table across a variable-cell step while atom moves and image shifts of both signs stay inside the Verlet skin, instead of rebuilding on every box change. Periodic coordinates are folded before the search, so whole-cell drift in the input keeps the same pairs; their shifts follow the coordinates as given. Each atom's move is measured from the image of its reference nearest it, so an atom that steps across a face of the cell, or that the caller wraps back into it, keeps a pair list.
