Potentials report per-atom energies. `RgpotCalculator` implements ASE's `energies` property (`get_potential_energies()`) for `lj`, `morse`, `eam_al`, `cuh2`, `stillinger_weber_si` and `tersoff_sic`, and the shares sum to `energy`. A pair term is split half and half, an EAM embedding term sits on the embedded atom and a three-body term on its centre atom; LAMMPS `compute pe/atom` splits three-body terms in thirds instead. In C++, `Potential::perAtomEnergies()` and `Potential::forceEnergies()` cover LJ, Morse, Sutton-Chen, the cell-list EAM, the Fortran EAM-Al and CuH2 kernels, Stillinger-Weber, Tersoff SiC and the TIP4P, SPC/E and TIP4P-Pt water models, thin periodic cells included. C callers use `rgpot_engine_force_energies` and `RgpotEngineCaps::per_atom_energy` (engine ABI minor 3). Energy-and-force calls are unchanged and do not compute them. `evaluate(..., energies=True)` returns them from the Python objects, and `TersoffSiC` gains `caps()` and `evaluate()`, so `tersoff_sic` now reports stress in ASE.
