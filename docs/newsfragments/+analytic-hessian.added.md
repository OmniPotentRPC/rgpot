A potential handle can register an analytic dense energy Hessian. `rgpot_potential_hessian` uses that callback, or builds the matrix from a registered analytic Hessian-vector product, and returns `RGPOT_NO_HESSIAN` when the potential has neither. It does not difference forces. `RGPOT_HVP_AUTO` multiplies a registered Hessian when no product callback is registered. Engines that already advertise `analytic_hvp` also serve `rgpot_engine_hessian` as those products on the unit directions. libxtb's `xtb_hessian` stays unadvertised: it is a numerical difference.
