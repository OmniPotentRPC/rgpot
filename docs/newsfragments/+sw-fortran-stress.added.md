The Fortran `SWPot` (`PotType::SWSi`) writes the Cauchy stress and `caps().stress` is true. It sums the same bond-vector virial as `StillingerWeberSi`, so its stress is the `StillingerWeberSi` stress times the epsilon ratio 1.068176741337. Its energies and forces keep their bits. `SWPot::forceBatch`, the call eOn's engine makes, skips the virial and leaves `has_stress` at 0. In Python, `StillingerWeberSi` gains `caps()` and `evaluate()`, which returns the stress, so `RgpotCalculator` reports the ASE stress for it and for `SWPot`.
