Added `GuptaPot` (`PotType::Gupta`), wrapping the Cleri-Rosato Gupta potential across the C++ `Potential` interface (with periodic minimum-image support, Cauchy stress, per-atom energy/virial, staged evaluation, and `rgpot_gupta_energy_forces` C ABI), the `pot_spec` / classical engine / `potserv` `"Gupta"` name, and Python/ASE `rgpot.GuptaPot` (`RgpotCalculator("gupta", ...)`). Coincident atoms are not a finite minimum: `rgpot_gupta` returns +infinity and `GuptaPot` poisons the forces with NaN, and a neighbour or staged call sets the energy and the forces to NaN. A NaN coordinate stays NaN. Zero hopping stays finite.
