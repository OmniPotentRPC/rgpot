Neighbour lists on sheared cells build about as fast as on an orthorhombic cell of the same density. A stored list for 11k Cu atoms on a sheared basis took about half a second per build and now takes the same tens of milliseconds as the cube; streamed walks, `rgpot_nlist_build` and the minimum-image fold are faster on sheared cells too. The pairs, their order, the separation vectors and the energies are unchanged to the bit.
