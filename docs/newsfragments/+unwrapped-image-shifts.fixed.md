A neighbour query on coordinates that lie whole cells outside the box no longer drops pairs. `rgpot::nlist::build`, `Builder`, `Walk` and `rgpot::lr::DualList` fold periodic coordinates in fractional space before they choose canonical image shifts. In a 10 Å box, atoms at 0 and 18.5 Å with a 2 Å cutoff now give their one pair, and adding further whole-cell offsets leaves the pair set unchanged. A periodic coordinate that is not finite, or lies more than 2^29 cells from the origin, is rejected with `Code::Box`. `rgpot::lr::DualList` rejects a non-finite position with `std::invalid_argument` ("positions must be finite").
