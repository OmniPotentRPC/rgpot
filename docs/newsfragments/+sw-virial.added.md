`ForceOut` carries a 3x3 virial in eV beside the Cauchy stress. `has_virial` is 0 when a potential does not compute it, including potentials that already write stress. When both are present, stress = -virial/V in eV/Angstrom^3, the ASE sign. The same tensors are optional on the C ABI (`rgpot_potential_calculate_mechanical`; the old calculate call is unchanged) and on `PotentialResult` (`virial`, `hasVirial`, `hasStress`). `StillingerWeberSi` fills both from the two-body and three-body terms as a sum of folded r_ij ⊗ f_ij.
