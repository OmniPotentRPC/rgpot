`tests/python/test_lammps_agreement.py` checks LJ, Morse, tabulated EAM (Mishin Cu 2001 and Al 1999 from NIST), Sutton-Chen, Stillinger-Weber, Tersoff SiC, ZBL and the Fortran SW, Tersoff, EDIP and Lenosky kernels against LAMMPS on orthorhombic, triclinic, thin and 4000-atom cells, comparing energy, forces, stress and per-atom energies. All agree to about 1e-13, except closed-form Sutton-Chen against its `eam/fs` tabulation, whose forces differ by up to 1.5e-9 from the table's first-order last interval. The module skips without the `lammps` Python module. The Python module gains `SuttonChenPot`, `ZBLPot`, `EAMTablePot` (when built with `-Dwith_eam_table=true`, flagged by `has_eam_table`), `StillingerWeberSi.params()` and `TersoffSiC.params()`.
