rgpot::nlist::Neighbours and nlist::build no longer miss pairs on strongly skewed or large triclinic cells, nor on an orthogonal cell whose atoms lie outside the box. The 27-bin stencil over Cartesian bins missed neighbours of a sheared cell, so the pair kernels, which read that list, dropped real pairs and returned wrong energies and forces. A sheared cell now takes its pairs and integer shifts from vesin, whose stencil follows the perpendicular face widths, and an orthogonal cell bins coordinates folded into the box.
