Periodic kernels count a neighbour at every periodic image inside the cutoff, not only the nearest one, so a cell thinner than twice the cutoff (a one-atom fcc cell, a triclinic primitive cell) gives the energy, forces and stress of an explicit supercell. This covers LJ, Morse, Sutton-Chen, Stillinger-Weber, the Tersoff SiC kernel, the cell-list EAM, the Fortran gathers (EAM-Al, FeHe, CuH2, SW, EDIP, Lenosky) and the TIP4P, SPC/E and TIP4P-Pt water models, through every entry point: the C ABI, the RPC server, eOn and the ASE calculators. EDIP no longer rejects a thin cell. Cells at least twice the cutoff thick keep the minimum-image path. `RgpotCalculator` no longer tiles thin cells itself, and `RgpotRpcCalculator` no longer takes a `cutoff`.
