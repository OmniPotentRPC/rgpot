`MEAMPot`, `D2Pot` and `D2CpmdPot` now bind the count set with `setThreadCount` on the potential while they evaluate. Their kernels start no OpenMP region of their own, but the neighbour list they build and every helper they call size their teams from the bound count, and none of the three bound it, so on a large system the setting had no effect on those steps. Each kernel now binds the count for the duration of the call: the potential's count sets the team, a `ThreadCount` scope on the call still wins, and a potential with no count keeps the `OMP_NUM_THREADS` team. Energy, forces and stress are bit for bit the same at every count.
