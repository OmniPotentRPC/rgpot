Potential evaluations can now request Cauchy stress, in eV/Angstrom^3, through the C++, C, Rust and Python interfaces, and over RPC. Stress is opt-in, through `ForceInput::compute_stress` in C++, the separate `rgpot_potential_calculate_stress` and `rgpot_rpc_calculate_stress` entry points in C, and `compute_stress=True` in Python, and a request does not change the energy or forces. `EAMPot` answers for orthorhombic cells, `TBLitePot` for GFN1 (from the strain derivative of its energy), and `EMTPot` from the strain derivative of its Asap energy. `XTBPot` does not compute stress and refuses a request, because its molecule is not periodic. `pair_style rgpot` requests stress when LAMMPS asks for the virial and the potential advertises it. `rgpot-eval` requests it from a potential that advertises it.
