`PotType::TersoffSi` is one potential. All-silicon configurations, and a null species pointer, use the Fortran gather. Any other atomic number uses the 1989 silicon-carbon kernel (`TersoffSiC`; Tersoff, Phys. Rev. B 39, 5566 (1989), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.39.5566). The erratum Phys. Rev. B 41, 3248 (1990), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.41.3248.2, sets carbon B to 346.74 eV and d to 4.3484. Carbon beta is dimensionless and is not printed by the erratum. An atomic number other than 6 or 14 is rejected. There is no separate silicon-carbide PotType. Calling `TersoffSiC` directly always evaluates the 1989 kernel. `energy_gradient` evaluates one configuration or a batch of configurations that share a cell and a species list.
