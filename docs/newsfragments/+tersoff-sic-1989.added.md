`TersoffSiC` is the Tersoff potential for silicon, carbon, and silicon-carbide from Tersoff, Phys. Rev. B 39, 5566 (1989), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.39.5566. The erratum Phys. Rev. B 41, 3248 (1990), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.41.3248.2, sets carbon B to 346.74 eV and d to 4.3484. Carbon beta is dimensionless and is not printed by the erratum. `energy_gradient` evaluates one configuration or a batch of configurations that share a cell and a species list.
