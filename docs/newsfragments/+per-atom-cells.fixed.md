The cell-list EAM accepts sheared cells, including symmetric primitive rows with zeros on the diagonal, and gives the right energies on lower-triangular supercells large enough to be binned rather than tiled (from 5x5x5 of the fcc primitive cell). In `Mode::Stream`, a team walk following a one-thread call on moved atoms no longer reuses a tile stencil built for the old coordinates. Per-atom energies sum to the total and follow the atoms across symmetric and lower-triangular cell forms, wrapping, atoms outside the cell, thin cells and slabs.
