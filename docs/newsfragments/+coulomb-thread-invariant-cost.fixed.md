The thread-invariant `CoulombPot` sums no longer cost more than the thread-count dependent ones they replaced. Every region now runs on the whole team, never a shorter one, so no spare worker spins beside a smaller team. One thread walks the fixed chunks through a single force row and adds the same terms in the same order as a team does. The Ewald wave sum gives each atom its own force sum instead of keeping a force row per chunk, and the SPME charge spread deposits straight into the grid planes a thread owns, with no private grid, no fold and no per-call buffers.
