Added `QSCPot` (`PotType::QSC`), the multi-element Quantum Sutton-Chen alloy potential of Qi, Cagin, Kimura and Goddard (Phys. Rev. B 59, 3527, 1999), Eqs. (1a) to (1c) with the combination rules of Eq. (2), with Cauchy stress, per-atom energies and virials, two-stage domain evaluation, exact analytic Hessian-vector products, `pot_spec` / `libclassical_engine` / `potserv` `"QSC"` support, and Python/ASE `rgpot.QSCPot` (`RgpotCalculator("qsc", ...)`). Pure elements agree with eOn's `QSC` class at commit 3b2586f to rounding; mixtures follow the published equations and differ from eOn 3b2586f, which caches pair parameters by one species and uses the neighbour's own density parameters (see the QSC reference page).
