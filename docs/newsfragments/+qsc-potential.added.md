Added `QSCPot` (`PotType::QSC`), the multi-element Quantum Sutton-Chen alloy potential of Qi, Cagin, Kimura and Goddard (Phys. Rev. B 59, 3527, 1999) and Rafii-Tabar and Sutton (Philos. Mag. Lett. 63, 217, 1991), with Cauchy stress, per-atom energies and virials, two-stage domain evaluation, exact analytic Hessian-vector products, `pot_spec` / `libclassical_engine` / `potserv` `"QSC"` support, and Python/ASE `rgpot.QSCPot` (`RgpotCalculator("qsc", ...)`).
