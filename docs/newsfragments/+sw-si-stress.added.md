`StillingerWeberSi::forceImpl` writes the Cauchy stress and `caps().stress` is true. The stress is (1/V) dE/dε in eV/Angstrom^3, with the sign and layout of the `LJPot`, `MorsePot` and `ZBLPot` stress. The virial sums each folded bond vector times the energy gradient with respect to that vector. This is the many-body form of Thompson, Plimpton and Mattson, J. Chem. Phys. 131, 154107 (2009), doi:10.1063/1.3245303. Energies and forces keep their bits. `SWSiTest` now also runs under CMake.
