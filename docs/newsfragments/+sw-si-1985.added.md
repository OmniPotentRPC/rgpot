`StillingerWeberSi` (`PotType::SWSi1985`) is the Stillinger-Weber silicon potential of Stillinger and Weber, Phys. Rev. B 31, 5262 (1985), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.31.5262. Epsilon is the 3.4723e-12 erg printed in equation (2.9), 2.16723920 eV. `PotType::SWSi` keeps eOn's rescaled epsilon, 2.16826 times 1.06767385, which is 2.3149945 eV. The ratio of the eOn epsilon to the published value is 1.068176741337. The factor 1.06767385 is copied from eOn's SWFortran.f90 and is untraced. `energy_gradient` evaluates one configuration or a batch of configurations that share a cell and a species list.
