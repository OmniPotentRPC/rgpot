`LJPot` takes an opt-in `tail_correction` flag on `LJConfig` (default `false`). On a fully periodic cell it adds the continuum long-range correction for the energy, the Cauchy stress and the per-atom energies and virials, and leaves the pair cutoff unshifted. With a nonzero `switch_width` the correction also restores the part of the bare 12-6 term that the quintic switch removes over `[cutoff - switch_width, cutoff]`, and the constructor throws `std::invalid_argument` when `switch_width` is not below `cutoff`. `kCxxSoversion` is bumped to 9 for the new `LJConfig` field.
