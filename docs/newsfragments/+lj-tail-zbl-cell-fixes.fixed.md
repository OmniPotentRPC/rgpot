`LJPot` integrates the quintic switch window in the normalised coordinate `u = (r - r_on) / width` over `[0, 1]` without polynomial cancellation, applies `tail_correction` on rotated and sheared periodic cells, and exposes `tail_correction` in `rgpot::spec::build("LJ", ...)` and the Python `rgpot.LJPot` binding. `ZBLPot` folds pairs across rotated periodic cells and rejects malformed non-zero cells with `std::invalid_argument`.
